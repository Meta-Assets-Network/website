## 中文版

**MetaAssets 隐私政策**

生效日期：[生效日期]

MA CHAIN GROUP LIMITED（"我们"）运营 MetaAssets 移动应用程序（"本 App"）。本政策说明我们收集、使用和保护个人信息的方式。使用本 App 即表示您同意本政策所述做法。

### 一、我们收集的信息

**1. 账户信息**
- 注册邮箱地址，用于账户注册、登录验证和身份确认。

**2. 钱包与链上数据**
- 钱包地址及公开链上交易记录（来自公链，非我们控制）。
- 您添加的自定义代币和网络配置。

**3. 即时通讯数据**
- 您主动发送的消息、图片、视频、语音等媒体文件（经您的操作上传至服务器用于转发给接收方）。
- 好友关系、群组成员身份、会话列表、备注名。

**4. 通话数据**
- 通话时长、通话类型（语音/视频）、通话对象标识，用于生成通话记录。通话内容经由实时音视频服务（Agora）传输。

**5. 设备与技术数据**
- 设备标识符、推送令牌（用于消息推送）、IP 地址、操作系统版本、语言设置。

**6. 交易与转账信息**
- 您发起的转账、红包所对应的链上交易哈希及关联的展示信息。

### 二、我们不收集的信息（重要）

- **私钥与助记词**：您的钱包私钥和助记词仅在您的设备上加密存储，**绝不会上传或传输给我们或任何第三方**。我们无法访问、恢复或托管您的数字资产。
- **生物识别原始数据**：面容 ID / 触控 ID 验证由设备本地完成，我们不收集生物特征信息。

### 三、信息的使用

我们收集的信息用于：
1. 提供账户注册、登录与安全验证；
2. 展示钱包余额、交易记录与资产信息；
3. 提供即时通讯、转账、红包及音视频通话功能；
4. 推送消息通知（新消息、转账、来电等）；
5. 保障 App 安全、防止欺诈与滥用；
6. 提供客户支持。

我们**不会**出售您的个人信息，**不会**将您的信息用于与 App 核心功能无关的目的。

### 四、第三方服务

为实现功能，本 App 使用以下第三方服务，它们按各自隐私政策处理必要数据：
- **推送服务**（EngageLab）：设备标识与推送令牌，用于消息送达。
- **实时音视频**（Agora）：通话信令与媒体传输。
- **Sign in with Apple**：由 Apple 提供的身份验证。
- **公链节点与区块浏览器**：查询公开链上数据。

### 五、信息的存储与安全

- 信息存储于我们的服务器并传输过程加密（TLS/SSL）。
- 我们仅在实现本政策所述目的所必需的期间保留数据。
- 我们采取合理的技术与管理措施保护您的信息，但请理解互联网传输不存在绝对安全。

### 六、您的权利

您可以：
- 通过 App 内功能删除会话记录、好友关系；
- 联系我们请求访问、更正或删除您的账户信息（删除账户将同步删除我们的服务器数据）；
- 在系统设置中关闭推送通知、相机、相册、麦克风等权限。

### 七、未成年人

本 App 不面向 18 岁以下未成年人，我们不会有意收集未成年人的个人信息。

### 八、政策更新

我们可能不时更新本政策，更新后将在 App 内或本页面公布。

### 九、联系我们

对本政策有任何疑问，请联系：[联系邮箱]

## English Version

**MetaAssets Privacy Policy**

Effective Date: [生效日期]

MA CHAIN GROUP LIMITED ("we") operates the MetaAssets mobile application (the "App"). This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect personal information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described herein.

### 1. Information We Collect

**1. Account Information**
- Email address used for registration, login verification, and identity confirmation.

**2. Wallet & On-Chain Data**
- Wallet addresses and public on-chain transaction records (derived from public blockchains, not controlled by us).
- Custom tokens and network configurations you add.

**3. Instant Messaging Data**
- Messages, images, videos, voice notes you actively send (uploaded to our servers for delivery to recipients upon your action).
- Friend relationships, group memberships, conversation lists, and remark names.

**4. Call Data**
- Call duration, call type (voice/video), and counterpart identifiers used to generate call history. Call media is transmitted via a real-time audio/video service (Agora).

**5. Device & Technical Data**
- Device identifiers, push tokens (for message delivery), IP address, OS version, and language settings.

**6. Transaction & Transfer Information**
- On-chain transaction hashes and associated display information for transfers and red packets you initiate.

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect (Important)

- **Private keys and mnemonics**: Your wallet private keys and seed phrases are encrypted and stored **on your device only** and are **never uploaded or transmitted to us or any third party**. We cannot access, recover, or custody your digital assets.
- **Raw biometric data**: Face ID / Touch ID verification is performed locally by your device; we do not collect biometric information.

### 3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:
1. Provide account registration, login, and security verification;
2. Display wallet balances, transaction records, and asset information;
3. Provide messaging, transfers, red packets, and audio/video calling;
4. Deliver push notifications (new messages, transfers, incoming calls);
5. Maintain App security and prevent fraud and abuse;
6. Provide customer support.

We do **not** sell your personal information or use it for purposes unrelated to the App's core functionality.

### 4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services to deliver functionality; each processes only the data necessary under its own privacy policy:
- **Push service** (EngageLab): device identifiers and push tokens for message delivery.
- **Real-time audio/video** (Agora): call signaling and media transport.
- **Sign in with Apple**: authentication provided by Apple.
- **Blockchain nodes & explorers**: querying public on-chain data.

### 5. Storage & Security

- Information is stored on our servers and encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL).
- We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy.
- We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed absolutely secure.

### 6. Your Rights

You may:
- Delete conversation history and friend relationships through in-app features;
- Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information (account deletion also removes your data from our servers);
- Disable push notifications, camera, photo library, and microphone permissions in system settings.

### 7. Minors

The App is not directed at users under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

### 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be announced in the App or on this page.

For questions about this policy, contact: [联系邮箱]
